CHEQUE BOUNCE

A cheque bounce occurs when a bank does not honour a cheque presented for payment.

A cheque may be returned for different reasons, such as insufficient funds, account-related issues, signature problems, or other banking reasons.

When a cheque is returned unpaid, the bank generally provides information about the reason for dishonour.

The person receiving the cheque should preserve important documents such as the cheque, bank return memo, payment records, agreement, invoices, and communications between the parties.

In certain circumstances, dishonour of a cheque issued towards a legally enforceable debt or liability can lead to legal proceedings.

Legal notice and filing requirements can involve specific time limits. Therefore, the applicable legal procedure and current limitation periods should be checked carefully.

For example, if a person receives a cheque as payment of an existing debt and the bank returns it because of insufficient funds, the recipient may consider the remedies available under applicable law.

The exact legal remedy depends on the reason for dishonour, purpose of the cheque, evidence, and applicable law.
