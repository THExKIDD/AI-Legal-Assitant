CYBER CRIME

Cyber crime refers to unlawful activities involving computers, mobile devices, networks, digital accounts, or the internet.

Examples may include online fraud, unauthorized access to accounts, identity theft, phishing, cyber stalking, and misuse of personal information.

A person who experiences a cyber incident should preserve relevant evidence.

Evidence may include screenshots, emails, messages, transaction details, phone numbers, website addresses, account information, and other relevant records.

The victim should avoid deleting important messages or digital evidence that may help establish what happened.

Depending on the incident, the matter may be reported through the appropriate cybercrime reporting mechanism or law-enforcement authority.

For example, if a person receives a fake banking link and money is fraudulently taken from their account, they should preserve transaction details and other evidence and promptly report the incident through appropriate channels.

The exact legal procedure depends on the nature of the cyber incident and applicable law.
