RIGHT TO INFORMATION (RTI)

The Right to Information framework allows citizens to seek certain information from public authorities.

Its purpose is to promote transparency and accountability in the functioning of public authorities.

A person may submit an RTI application seeking information or records that are held by or under the control of the relevant public authority, subject to applicable exemptions.

The application should clearly describe the information being requested so that the authority can identify the relevant records.

The applicant should submit the request to the appropriate public authority and follow the applicable procedure.

Some categories of information may be exempt from disclosure under the law.

If an applicant does not receive an appropriate response, appeal mechanisms may be available according to the applicable RTI procedure.

For example, a citizen may seek available records concerning the status or processing of a matter handled by a public authority.

The exact procedure, fees, exemptions, and time limits should be checked under the applicable RTI rules.
