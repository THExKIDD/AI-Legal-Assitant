RENTAL DISPUTE

A rental dispute is a disagreement between a landlord and a tenant regarding rented property.

Disputes may involve rent payments, security deposits, property damage, maintenance, possession, termination of tenancy, or other terms of the rental arrangement.

A written rent or lease agreement is important because it records the rights and responsibilities agreed between the landlord and tenant.

Both parties should preserve relevant documents such as the rental agreement, rent receipts, payment records, notices, photographs, and messages.

If a disagreement occurs, the parties may first try to resolve it through communication.

Depending on the circumstances, a written notice or an appropriate legal remedy may be required.

For example, if there is a disagreement about the return of a security deposit, the rental agreement and payment records can be important evidence.

Rental laws and procedures may vary depending on the location, type of property, agreement, and circumstances.
