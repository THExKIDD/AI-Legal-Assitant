CONTRACT

A contract is an agreement between two or more parties that creates legal obligations.

Contracts may be written, oral, or created through conduct, depending on the circumstances and applicable law.

A valid contract generally involves an offer by one party and acceptance by another party. The parties should agree to the terms and there should be lawful consideration and a lawful purpose.

The parties entering into a contract must also have the legal capacity to enter into the agreement.

Important terms of a written contract may include the names of the parties, responsibilities of each party, payment terms, time period, conditions, and consequences of breach.

A breach of contract occurs when a party fails to perform an obligation required by the contract.

Depending on the circumstances, the affected party may seek an appropriate legal remedy.

For example, if a person agrees to provide a service for payment but fails to provide the promised service, a contractual dispute may arise.

The rights and remedies available depend on the terms of the agreement, facts of the case, and applicable law.
